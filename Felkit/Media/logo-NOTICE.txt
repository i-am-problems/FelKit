logo.tga (128 px) and logo-small.tga (32 px) are FelKit's logo: a green fel crystal
with an orbit ring on a dark disc. It is original art made for FelKit, the same image
used on its CurseForge page, cut to a circle for the in-game seal. No game art is used.
Uncompressed 32-bit TGA, bottom-left origin.
