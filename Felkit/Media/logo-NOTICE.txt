logo.tga (128 px) and logo-small.tga (32 px) are the add-on's seal: a local
composition of World of Warcraft icons inside a drawn gold and violet ring.

Icons used (Blizzard Entertainment, Inc.):
- Interface/Icons/ClassIcon_Warlock (file 626007, original 64 px client art, rim cropped)
- Spell_Shadow_SoulGem, Spell_Shadow_AbominationExplosion, Spell_Shadow_Twilight,
  INV_Stone_04 (56 px renders from the public Wowhead icon CDN)

trademarks of Blizzard Entertainment, Inc. in the U.S. and/or other countries.
The icons remain Blizzard's; the add-on's MIT license covers its code only and is
not asserted to relicense this art.

Local transformation: smooth bicubic upscale, circular masks, ring drawn locally,
downscaled with Lanczos; uncompressed 32-bit TGA, bottom-left origin.
